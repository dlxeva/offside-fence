# Crowdfunding Platform Project Review Checklist

> **This document is a learning reference, not part of the OffsideFence project.**
> **本文件是学习参考，不是 OffsideFence 项目的一部分。**
>
> It is saved here as a quiet corner of the repository: a record of the standard milestone-based review framework used by major crowdfunding platforms (Kickstarter, Indiegogo style) when evaluating creator projects, captured for the day — if ever — when the author builds something real enough to go through this process.
>
> 保存为仓库的安静角落：记录主流众筹平台（Kickstarter / Indiegogo 风）在评估创作者项目时使用的标准里程碑审核框架。留给未来——如果作者要做真的产品而走完这套流程——作为预习资料。

## Stage 0 — Refine your strategy / 制定策略

**Confirm your milestones and plan your follow-up actions.**
**確認勝利果實以及後續動作。**

Re-examine the different stages of product development, and mark what has been completed so far. Your description of the project's progress should be honest. These milestones let you and your backers know what to expect at every stage of the project.

再次檢視產品開發的不同階段，並標出到目前為止已經完成的內容。對專案進展的描述應當誠實。這些里程碑能讓你瞭解我們和支持者在專案的每個階段的期望。

> **Note from the author:** Once you publish a project, you cannot edit this content. The platform takes a snapshot of your milestones at launch time. Plan carefully.

---

## Stage 1 — Concept & Business Plan / 概念与商业计划

**You should have / 你应当拥有的:**

- 你的产品想法的详细描述 / A detailed description of your product idea
- 高级发展计划 / A high-level development plan

**We and your backers expect / 我们和你的支持者期望的:**

一份商业计划，其中包含：
- 此产品如何解决问题 / How this product solves a problem
- 谁将从中受益 / Who will benefit
- 你的预算和分發計劃 / Your budget and distribution plan
- 潛在的風險，包含法律和監管風險 / Potential risks, including legal and regulatory risks
- 產品的早期草圖或模型 / Early sketches or models of the product
- 支持你的結論的相關研究 / Relevant research supporting your conclusions

---

## Stage 2 — Early Prototypes / 早期原型

> The platform wants you to share your creation process. Multiple early prototypes, for multiple purposes — showing how the idea becomes real. They don't have to be perfect; they should help you validate early assumptions. Materials can be cardboard, tape, laser-cut wood, metal, or 3D-printed acrylic.

**You should have / 你应当拥有的:**

- 展示技術層面如何運作的功能原型 / A functional prototype showing how the technical layer works
- 展示形狀和感覺的外觀原型 / A look-and-feel prototype showing the form factor

**We and your backers expect / 我们和你的支持者期望的:**

- 介紹原型的照片、圖畫、影片和其他媒體資料 / Photos, drawings, videos, and other media introducing the prototypes
- 關於旅程如何展開的最新情況，包括遇到的挑戰 / Updates on how the journey is unfolding, including challenges

---

## Stage 3 — Single Working Prototype / 单个工作原型

> Sharing your creation process with backers helps them provide valuable feedback. You need a single prototype that looks and works close to the final product. The way you make this prototype can differ from the final production method, but you should be ready with the technology you will use in production.

**You should have / 你应当拥有的:**

- 一個能工作且看起來像最終產品的單一原型 / A single prototype that works and looks like the final product
- 對製造和裝配方案的初步審查 / A preliminary review of manufacturing and assembly options
- 關於哪家公司將生產它的想法 / An idea of which company will produce it

**We and your backers expect / 我们和你的支持者期望的:**

- 設計過程的文件 / Documentation of the design process
- 在您的宣傳活動描述中用於概述原型的所有特徵和功能的未經剪輯的演示影片 / Unedited demo videos outlining all the features and functions of the prototype in your campaign description

---

## Stage 4 — DFM Prototype (Design for Manufacturing) / 可制造化设计原型

> This is often the riskiest stage. Creators who go further have a greater chance of successfully fulfilling. To minimize risk, consider delaying your campaign launch until you have produced a DFM prototype.

**You must show / 你必须展示的:**

- 工作原型，體現將產品作為回報供應的能力 / A working prototype demonstrating the ability to deliver the product as a reward

> When setting up shipping and supply chain options, consider whether you need outside expertise. This is a critical milestone. You need a prototype whose appearance, function, and method of manufacture all match the final product.

**You should have / 你应当拥有的:**

- 產品規格的最終版本 / Final version of the product specification
- 組件和材料的最終清單，以簡化生產的複雜性並優化成本，並製定更現實的預算 / Final list of components and materials, to simplify production complexity and optimize cost, and to develop a more realistic budget
- 與製造合作夥伴就已知的最小訂單量簽訂協議 / Signed agreement with a manufacturing partner on known minimum order quantities
- 完成監管和/或合規性測試的詳細計劃 / A detailed plan for completing regulatory and/or compliance testing

**We and your backers expect / 我们和你的支持者期望的:**

- 產品演進的文件 / Documentation of product evolution
- DFM 原型的照片和演示影片 / Photos and demo videos of the DFM prototype
- 包裝設計 / Packaging design
- 關於你遇到的挑戰和經驗教訓的持續溝通 / Ongoing communication about challenges encountered and lessons learned

---

## Stage 5 — Pre-production / 试生产

> You should have a realistic understanding of the production timeline and cost estimates. Be prepared to face challenges in finalizing the production process, certification and compliance, and shipping logistics. Components and machines for manufacturing are ready, and DFM prototypes can be mass-produced continuously.

**You should have / 你应当拥有的:**

- 流程和工具已到位，可輕鬆複製產品 / Processes and tools in place to easily replicate the product
- 與製造合作夥伴共同完成多次的試運行和品質保證 / Multiple trial runs and quality assurance completed with manufacturing partners
- 相信你的產品符合品質標準，投入市場也是完全合法的 / Confidence that your product meets quality standards and that bringing it to market is fully legal

**We and your backers expect / 我们和你的支持者期望的:**

- 預生產原型的照片和未經剪輯的演示影片 / Photos and unedited demo videos of pre-production prototypes
- 模具和生產設備的照片 / Photos of molds and production equipment
- 與製造合作夥伴共同完成多次的試運行和品質保證的影片 / Videos of multiple trial runs and quality assurance completed with manufacturing partners

---

## Stage 6 — Mass Production & Fulfillment / 量产与履约

> Be aware of potential supply chain and logistics obstacles. This may include material sourcing, travel restrictions, and labor issues, all of which can affect your fulfillment plan. You have successfully produced and assembled all the rewards, including add-ons.

**We and your backers expect / 我们和你的支持者期望的:**

- 在工廠車間或在準備發貨的包裝中的回報的照片或影片 / Photos or videos of the rewards in the factory floor or in packaging ready to be shipped

> Be prepared for possible changes in cost, customs, or other logistics that could delay your reward shipments.

## Reflection / 复盘备注

**Author's notes for future-self, captured 2026-06-16:**

- Stages 0-2 are about *intent and ideation* — the cheapest place to fail.
- Stage 3 is where most crowdfunding projects die — the "single working prototype" stage is where the gap between "concept" and "manufacturable" becomes unforgiving.
- Stage 4 (DFM) is the real moat. Most projects skip it and pay for it later.
- Stages 5-6 are operational, not creative. By then, the design decisions are made; you're just executing.

**If OffsideFence were ever to take this path**, the project would have to fail all five of its current boundary declarations:
1. ❌ not a product
2. ❌ not affiliated with Roboflow
3. ❌ not a content series
4. ❌ not bound to specific players
5. ❌ not a critique of VAR

**It won't. This file is a hypothetical, kept for the day it might be relevant to a different project.**
